Solar Installation

From Site to Switch-On

Rooftop vs Utility-Scale

Rooftop (1–10 kW): Installed on existing roofs, mostly for self-use and net metering. About 10 m² per kW; installation can take days.

Utility-scale (MW+): Ground-mounted on about 4–5 acres per MW, with high-voltage connection to substations. Projects take months.
